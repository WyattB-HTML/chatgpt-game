# Cursed Keep: Cards and Enemies

This reference matches the six-room game data. Each hero deck contains the same 10 Basic Strikes and 10 Blocks, plus 20 cards unique to that class, for 40 cards total. “Movement value” is the number of tiles a card lets its owner move when discarded; it is not the card’s attack range or any charge effect. Hover text follows the current in-game keyword tooltip. The `[player name]` placeholder is replaced by the active hero’s name; text values below use base values before class passives or room effects change range or area.

## Enemies

HP is the enemy’s base value. Actual maximum HP scales by room and party size: `ceil(base HP × [1 + 0.08 × (players − 2) + 0.025 × (room − 1)])`. The Giant Skeleton King appears in Room 6; at two players, it has 33 HP after Room 6 scaling. Each enemy draws from its own 15-card deck; card counts are shown in parentheses.

| Skeletal Guard | Bone Spear (8): 1 damage, range 1; Brace (4): gain 2 Guard; Shield Jab (3): 1 damage, range 1, then push 1 tile. | 5 |
| Skeletal Soldier | Graveblade Cut (7): 2 damage, range 1; Raise the Dead (3): gain 2 Guard and give 1 Guard to the most wounded undead ally within 4 tiles; Driving Blow (5): 2 damage, range 1, then push 1 tile. | 7 |

| Zombie Hound | Rotting Bite (7): 1 damage, range 1, and root the target; Grave Pounce (5): 2 damage, closes to range 1, then pushes 1 tile; Death Rattle (3): 1 damage, range 2, and weaken the target. | 4 |
