How to get in
and get out.

The job

Two to six heroes work through 12 rooms across two castle floors. Defeat every foe in a room, share the rewards, beat the King in Room 6 and a vault guardian in Room 12, then reach the red gate with the relic.

A round of combat

On your turn, draw up to 3 cards. You may play up to 2 cards: move, attack, defend, heal, or use a skill. Unplayed cards stay in your hand. Used cards go to your discard pile; when your draw pile is empty, shuffle your discard pile. Out-of-turn cards trigger automatically when their printed condition occurs. When everyone ends their turn, the castle company moves and attacks.

The map & fighting

Tap a card, then choose a tile or target on the grid. Distance is counted in horizontal and vertical tiles. Walls block movement unless a card says jump or teleport. Guard absorbs damage before HP. At 0 HP a hero falls; a fallen hero cannot act.

Growing stronger

After every room, each living hero chooses one of three passive abilities. The crew shares a draft of items: there is one item per two heroes (rounded up). Permanent items stay with their carrier; consumables are spent when used. After Room 6, each hero also chooses one of two subclasses and their unique cards upgrade.

Four strange rooms

Rooms 3, 6, 9, and 12 reveal one random static effect. Each effect gives the crew a boon with a drawback. Read the banner before choosing your cards.

The Rogue (optional)

With Rogue mode on, one seat in a crew of 3–6 may choose Rogue and imitate a class no one else uses. Flip any unique card into a Rogue trick. In Room 12, stand beside the living guardian to try to steal the relic. A Rogue carrying it must escape alone to win; otherwise the crew wins by reaching the exit together.

Join across devices

Create a room and share its five-letter code or invite link. Friends can join from their own phone or computer. The host starts when at least two players are in.
