How to get in
and get out.

The job

Two to six heroes work through 12 rooms across two castle floors. Defeat every foe in a room, share the rewards, beat the King in Room 6 and a vault guardian in Room 12, then reach the red gate with the relic.

A round of combat

At the start of your turn, draw up to your hand size (3 by default). You may use 2 card plays. Playing or discarding a card uses one play; unplayed cards stay in your hand. A card effect that draws a card also grants one extra play per card drawn this turn. Used cards go to your discard; when the draw pile is empty, the discard pile is shuffled back in. Enemies each draw and play a card from their own 15-card deck when the crew ends the round.

The map & fighting

Tap a card, then choose a highlighted tile or target on the grid. Distance uses the greater of horizontal or vertical steps: a diagonal is 1 tile away. Movement and range count diagonals as one. Walls and other fighters block movement unless a card says jump or teleport. Guard absorbs damage before HP. At 0 HP a hero falls; a fallen hero cannot act.

Growing stronger & loot

After every room, each living hero chooses one of three passive abilities. Items equal to half the party size, rounded up, are drafted in a rotating order. On your pick, choose an item and assign it to any living hero, or pass. The first picker rotates each room. Permanent items stay with their carrier; consumables are spent when used. After Room 6, each hero also chooses one of two subclasses and their unique cards upgrade.

Four strange rooms

Rooms 3, 6, 9, and 12 reveal one random static effect. Each effect gives the crew a boon with a drawback. Read the banner before choosing your cards.

The Rogue (optional)

The host can opt into Rogue mode before starting a party of 3–6. All classes, including a possible Rogue, are dealt randomly at start; no two heroes receive the same class. A Rogue imitates an unused class and can flip unique cards into Rogue tricks. In Room 12, the Rogue may try to steal the relic. A Rogue carrying it must escape alone to win; otherwise the crew wins by reaching the exit together.

Join across devices

Create a room and share its five-letter code or invite link. Friends can join from their own phone or computer. The host starts when at least two players are in.
